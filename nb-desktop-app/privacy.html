Privacy policy

Nipun Batra · Personal desktop integration · 10 September 2026

nb-desktop-app is operated by Nipun Batra for personal use. It accesses Google account information and the Google Workspace data needed for tasks Nipun requests, within the permissions granted through Google OAuth.

For email workflows, this can include message headers, message bodies, and attachments. Account information identifies the connected account. Retrieved content may be processed by locally configured assistants, including OpenAI Codex, to complete the requested work. Their applicable account settings and service policies govern that processing.

The local Google Workspace CLI stores OAuth credentials encrypted, using the operating system keyring. Downloaded content and generated documents may be retained in Nipun's chosen local or cloud-synced folders until removed. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously downloaded files or assistant conversation history.

This integration does not publish mailbox contents through its documentation website. Publication or sharing of task outputs is a separate user-directed action. Google user data is not sold or used by this integration for advertising.

The integration's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked in Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Contact: nipun.batra@iitgn.ac.in.
