nb-desktop-app

Nipun Batra · Personal desktop integration · 10 September 2026

nb-desktop-app is Nipun Batra's personal desktop integration for Google Workspace. It connects locally installed command-line tools and assistants to Nipun's authorized Google accounts for tasks such as finding email, reviewing receipts, and preparing personal work documents. It is not offered as a public service.

Access depends on the permissions approved in Google's consent screen. The current personal Gmail connection permits reading and managing email. Actions are initiated through Nipun's local tools and instructions.
