# Refined index (selected October 2026)

Reference mockups for the chosen reorganisation of Home, Teaching and Open source.
Swiss red only; the palette rules in `palettes.css` other than the toggle are unused.

- `a-home.html`: full biography, research and teaching side by side, conversations after them.
- `a-teaching.html`: search, jump links, this-semester feature, one row per course with semester chips, full-width playlist grid, cheatsheets listed once.
- `a-projects.html`: Open source in the same layout, with tags and search.
- `theme.js` and the `.mode` rules in `palettes.css`: the compact sun and moon light/dark switch (follows the system until clicked).
- `build.py`: generator used for the mockups (reads `data/teaching/catalog.json`); image paths point at `img/`, which maps to `images/` here.
